Glossary

What the gear words on this site mean.

The short answer

  • 77 words explained in plain English.
  • Each meaning is written from a saved source. Tap "+ Source" to see it.
  • Words in item details link here when they appear.
  • On a phone, tap a letter to open its words.
  • Meanings are plain facts. Where a reviewer adds an opinion about a word, it is shown apart as "Reviewer view", with its label.

#3 words: 60% layout, 75% layout, 96% layout

60% layout

The smallest common layout: no number pad, function row, navigation keys or arrow keys.

Source: Tom's Hardware - Best Gaming Keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

75% layout

A compact keyboard with no number pad and fewer navigation keys, but it keeps the arrow keys and function row.

Source: Tom's Hardware - Best Gaming Keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

96% layout

A squashed full-size keyboard. It keeps the number pad but drops some navigation keys.

Source: Tom's Hardware - Best Gaming Keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
A3 words: actuation point, analog input, Armoury Crate

actuation point

How far down you must press a key before it registers.

Source: PC Gamer - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

analog input

The keyboard tracks how far each key is pressed, not just on or off. This allows features like Rapid Trigger and finer control.

Source: Wooting - 80HE product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

Armoury Crate

ASUS's app for setting up its keyboards and other gear. For the ROG Strix Scope II 96 Wireless keyboard on this site, ASUS also offers a lighter app, Armoury Crate Gear.

Source: RTINGS review - ASUS ROG Strix Scope II 96 Wireless (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
B1 word: Banana switch

Banana switch

One of the switch versions Keychron sells, next to Red and Brown. Tom's Guide lists it as a tactile switch (you feel a small bump when the key presses).

Reviewer view: Tom's Guide says the Banana switches it tested on the V1 Ultra 8K have a lovely tactile feel, though they are on the heavy side. Opinion: Tom's Guide

Source: Keychron - K2 Ultra 8K (product data) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Keychron - Q6 Ultra 8K (product data) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Tom's Guide - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: Tom's Guide - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
C5 words: click and sensor latency, constant motion, control pad, Cordura, CPI / DPI

click and sensor latency

The small delay between your click or movement and the PC seeing it. Low latency makes a gaming mouse feel responsive.

Source: RTINGS - The Best Mouse of 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

constant motion

How Logitech tests battery life: with the mouse moving all the time. Logitech says real battery life varies. Other makers test in other ways, so their hours do not compare.

Source: Logitech G - PRO X SUPERLIGHT 2 product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

control pad

A mouse pad made to help you stop the mouse exactly where you want.

Reviewer view: DiamondLobby suggests control pads for tactical shooters like CS2 and Valorant. Opinion: DiamondLobby

Source: DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (0:41) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (1:07) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (0:41) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

Cordura

A tough, durable fabric used on some mouse pads instead of normal cloth. DiamondLobby's auto-captions spell it "Cura".

Reviewer view: DiamondLobby finds Cordura more abrasive than a normal cloth pad. Opinion: DiamondLobby

Reviewer view: boardzy says older Cordura pads felt aggressive, and the market has moved to smoother, quieter Cordura. Opinion: boardzy

Source: DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (5:25) (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy); boardzy - 2024 Mousepad Tier List (19:36) (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (5:51) (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: boardzy - 2024 Mousepad Tier List (19:44) (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)

CPI / DPI

How far the cursor moves for each inch you move the mouse. A higher number moves the cursor further.

Source: PC Gamer - Best gaming mouse 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
D4 words: Darkfield, DKS, donut skates (donuts), dot skates (dots)

Darkfield

Logitech's name for the sensor in the MX Master 4. Logitech says the mouse tracks on any surface, including glass.

Source: Logitech - MX Master 4 product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

DKS

Short for 'dynamic keystrokes'. Lets one key do different things on a half press, full press and release.

Source: Wooting - 80HE product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

donut skates (donuts)

Skates similar to dots that you also place yourself. Wasabi says a pack has 32, and you change the speed by how many you use.

Source: Wasabi - Beginners Guide to aftermarket skates (2:32) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

dot skates (dots)

Small round skates you stick on the bottom of a mouse yourself, instead of full-size skates. A pack has about 40. Using fewer dots makes the glide faster; more dots give more control. 'Universal' dots fit most mice.

Source: Wasabi - Beginners Guide to aftermarket skates (1:51) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); JP Gaming USA store - X-raypad Obsidian Universal Dots (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)
E1 word: ergonomic shape

ergonomic shape

A mouse shaped for one hand (usually the right) so your hand can rest on it.

Source: RTINGS review - Razer DeathAdder V4 Pro (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); PC Gamer - Best gaming mouse 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
F4 words: Focus Pro, FPS, front-heavy, full size

Focus Pro

Razer's name for its mouse sensors. The number (like 50K) is the top DPI setting.

Source: Razer - Viper V4 Pro (Black) full spec list (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy); Razer - DeathAdder V4 Pro (Black) full spec list (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)

FPS

Short for first-person shooter: fast games where you aim through your character's eyes, such as Overwatch 2 or Apex Legends.

Source: Tom's Hardware - Best Gaming Keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Tom's Hardware - Best Gaming Mouse 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

front-heavy

When more of a mouse's weight sits toward its front instead of its middle.

Reviewer view: RTINGS says this may be noticeable on the X3 SUPERSTRIKE if you use low sensitivity, make broad sweeps or often lift the mouse. Opinion: RTINGS

Source: RTINGS review - Logitech G PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: RTINGS review - Logitech G PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

full size

A keyboard that includes the separate number pad on the right.

Source: Tom's Guide - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
G4 words: G HUB, gasket mount, glass pad, grip

G HUB

Logitech's app for its gaming mice and other gaming gear. Logitech says advanced features need it.

Source: Logitech G - G502 X PLUS product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Logitech G - PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

gasket mount

A way of mounting the inside of a keyboard that gives typing a softer, slightly springy feel.

Source: RTINGS review - Ducky OK-M (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

glass pad

A hard mouse pad with a glass surface instead of cloth.

Reviewer view: DiamondLobby says glass pads are fast, need little care and keep feeling new. Opinion: DiamondLobby

Source: DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (4:21) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (4:53) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (4:21) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (4:53) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

grip

How you hold a mouse. The common grips are palm, claw and fingertip.

Source: PC Gamer - Best gaming mouse 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
H6 words: Hall effect switches, haptic, HERO 2, hot-swappable, humidity resistance, HyperSpeed

Hall effect switches

A kind of magnetic switch. Magnets and sensors detect how far each key is pressed, so you can set the actuation point of each key.

Source: RTINGS - The Best Keyboards (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

haptic

A small motor makes the click you feel, instead of a mechanical part.

Source: RTINGS review - Logitech G PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

HERO 2

Logitech's name for the sensor in its newer gaming mice. HERO is the older version.

Source: Logitech G - PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Logitech G - G305 LIGHTSPEED product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

hot-swappable

You can pull out and change the key switches without soldering.

Source: RTINGS - The Best Gaming Keyboards (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Tom's Guide - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

humidity resistance

How well a pad keeps the same feel when the air is damp or your hands sweat.

Source: DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (1:30) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

HyperSpeed

Razer's name for its wireless connection. The Viper V4 Pro and DeathAdder V4 Pro list 'HyperSpeed Wireless Gen-2'.

Source: Razer - Viper V4 Pro (Black) full spec list (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy); Razer - DeathAdder V4 Pro (Black) full spec list (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)
I2 words: inductive clicks (HITS), IPS

inductive clicks (HITS)

Mouse buttons that sense a press with inductive sensing instead of a normal microswitch. A small motor gives the click feel. You can set how far a press must go before it counts.

Source: RTINGS - The Best Gaming Mouse (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Logitech G - PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE features and spec data (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)

IPS

The fastest mouse movement (in inches per second) the sensor can follow accurately.

Source: PC Gamer - Best gaming mouse 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
K1 word: Kailh Box V2

Kailh Box V2

A switch model made by the company Kailh. Tom's Guide lists White, Red and Brown versions for the Keychron V1 Ultra 8K.

Source: Tom's Guide - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
L6 words: lift-off distance, lighting zones, LIGHTSPEED, linear switch, Logi Bolt, low-profile

lift-off distance

How high you can lift the mouse before the sensor stops tracking.

Source: PC Gamer - Best gaming mouse 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

lighting zones

The number of areas a maker lists for a product's RGB lighting. Razer lists 15 zones for the Firefly V2 Pro.

Source: Razer - Firefly V2 Pro (Black) full spec list (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)

LIGHTSPEED

Logitech's name for the wireless connection on its gaming mice. It uses a small USB receiver.

Source: Logitech G - PRO X SUPERLIGHT 2 product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

linear switch

A key switch that moves straight down smoothly, with no bump and no click.

Source: PC Gamer - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

Logi Bolt

Logitech's small USB receiver for its work mice and keyboards.

Source: Logitech - MX Keys S product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); RTINGS review - Logitech MX Master 4 (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

low-profile

A keyboard or switch with a shorter build and shallower key travel than standard mechanical switches.

Source: RTINGS - The Best Keyboards (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); RTINGS review - Logitech MX Keys S (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
M4 words: magnetic switches, mAh, mention, mouse skates

magnetic switches

Key switches that use a small magnet to sense a press, without parts touching. This lets you set the actuation point of each key yourself.

Source: Tom's Guide - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); RTINGS - The Best Keyboards (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

mAh

The unit makers use to give a battery's size, for example 650 mAh on the MX Master 4 or 4000 mAh on Keychron boards. It does not tell you the hours by itself.

Source: Logitech - MX Master 4 product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Keychron - K2 Ultra 8K (product data) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

mention

When an outlet names an item as an alternative to its main picks (RTINGS calls these 'Notable Mentions'), or says something good about it in passing. It is a good sign, but it is not one of the outlet's main picks. We show it as "Listed as an alternative" and do not count it as a recommendation.

Source: RTINGS - The Best Gaming Mouse (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

mouse skates

Replacement feet for the bottom of your mouse. Full skates match the shape of the stock feet; dots are small round pieces you place yourself.

Source: Wasabi - Beginners Guide to aftermarket skates (1:24) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Wasabi - Beginners Guide to aftermarket skates (1:51) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
N1 word: notched and free-spin scrolling

notched and free-spin scrolling

Two scroll-wheel modes: notched moves in small clicky steps, free-spin rolls freely and much faster. RTINGS says the MX Master 4's top wheel can switch between them.

Source: RTINGS review - Logitech MX Master 4 (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
O3 words: onboard memory, optical switches, outlet

onboard memory

Settings profiles saved on the mouse itself. Logitech says the G502 X PLUS holds 5 profiles (2 by default, 5 if you use its G HUB app).

Source: Logitech G - G502 X PLUS product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

optical switches

A type of mouse button switch, used instead of the older mechanical kind.

Reviewer view: RTINGS says optical switches are usually more durable and do not develop double-click problems, but they feel stiffer at first and can get mushy as they age. Opinion: RTINGS

Source: RTINGS review - Logitech G PRO X SUPERLIGHT 2 (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: RTINGS review - Logitech G PRO X SUPERLIGHT 2 (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

outlet

Our word for one review site or reviewer, like RTINGS, PC Gamer or boardzy. When an outlet puts an item on its best-of list or in its top group, we say it "recommends" the item. Each outlet counts once, even if it recommends an item in two lists.

Source: This site - how we count (our own rule)
P5 words: PBT and ABS keycaps, PCR ABS and zinc alloy, PixArt sensors (PAW3395, PMW3391), polling rate, PTFE

PBT and ABS keycaps

The two plastics most keycaps are made from. Some boards come with either one.

Reviewer view: PC Gamer links PBT keycaps to a premium feel. Opinion: PC Gamer

Reviewer view: RTINGS says the K70 CORE's ABS keycaps feel a bit cheap. Opinion: RTINGS

Source: PC Gamer - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); RTINGS review - Corsair K70 CORE (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: PC Gamer - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: RTINGS review - Corsair K70 CORE (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

PCR ABS and zinc alloy

The two case materials Wooting sells for the 80HE: PCR ABS is a plastic, zinc alloy is a metal. Wooting lists the board at 790 g in PCR ABS and 2160 g in zinc alloy.

Source: Wooting - 80HE tech specifications (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)

PixArt sensors (PAW3395, PMW3391)

Model names of mouse sensors made by the company PixArt. The number is the model. An 'SE' version is a different sensor from the plain one.

Source: RTINGS review - VXE R1 Pro (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); ATK store - VXE Dragonfly R1 series model comparison (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy); TechPowerUp - Corsair KATAR PRO XT review (checked 2026-11-19, raw copy)

polling rate

How often a mouse or keyboard sends its input to the PC each second. 8000 Hz (8K) means once every 0.125 milliseconds.

Source: Wooting - 80HE product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Logitech G - PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

PTFE

The slick plastic most mouse feet are made of. Pure PTFE has no other materials mixed in.

Reviewer view: Wasabi says pure PTFE is soft, so it wears quickly on glass pads. Opinion: Wasabi

Source: Razer - Basilisk V3 product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Wasabi - Beginners Guide to aftermarket skates (3:51) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: Wasabi - Beginners Guide to aftermarket skates (3:51) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
R6 words: Rapid Trigger, raw performance, receiver (dongle), recommended, reviewer, RGB

Rapid Trigger

A feature where a key (or mouse click) resets as soon as you lift it a little, instead of at a fixed reset point.

Reviewer view: RTINGS says it is useful for quick repeated presses and direction changes in FPS and rhythm games, and matters less for casual gaming. Opinion: RTINGS

Source: RTINGS - The Best Gaming Keyboards (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: RTINGS - The Best Gaming Keyboards (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

raw performance

A heading RTINGS uses for speed results. RTINGS explains it for mice: in its Viper V4 Pro review, exceptional raw performance means very low click and sensor latency (delay). The detailed numbers are for RTINGS members only.

Source: RTINGS review - Razer Viper V4 Pro (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

receiver (dongle)

The small plug in your PC that a wireless mouse or keyboard talks to. Some receivers allow higher polling rates than others.

Source: RTINGS review - Razer DeathAdder V4 Pro (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

recommended

We count an item as recommended when the outlet itself presents it as a recommendation: a place on its best-of list, a clear "my recommendation is..." in a video, or a tier that the reviewer says he recommends (boardzy says he recommends anything in A tier or above). Items an outlet lists as alternatives, like RTINGS' "Notable Mentions", are not counted. Each outlet counts once.

Source: RTINGS - The Best Mouse of 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); boardzy - 2025 Mouse Skate Tier List (24:19) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); boardzy - 2024 Mousepad Tier List (52:04) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

reviewer

A person, site or lab that tests products. Each reviewer or site counts as one outlet.

Source: This site - how we count (our own rule)

RGB

Colour lighting on a mouse, keyboard or pad that you can change.

Source: PC Gamer - Best gaming mouse 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Tom's Hardware - Best Mouse Pads 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
S9 words: scissor switches, Silk POM, SOCD, source, spec score, starting friction, stock feet, stopping power, symmetrical shape

scissor switches

Thin, laptop-style key switches. They usually feel softer and travel a shorter distance than mechanical switches.

Source: RTINGS - The Best Keyboards (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); RTINGS review - Logitech MX Keys S (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

Silk POM

A switch model made by Keychron. Keychron sells it in Brown, Red and Banana versions.

Source: Keychron - Q6 Ultra 8K (product data) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

SOCD

A setting that decides what happens when you press two opposite keys at once, like left and right.

Source: RTINGS - The Best Gaming Keyboards (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); Wooting - 80HE product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

source

Any page or video we saved and used: reviews, best-of lists, videos and maker pages. Maker pages back specs and prices, but never count as an opinion or a pick.

Source: This site - how we count (our own rule)

spec score

Our number from 0 to 100 for mice and keyboards. For each spec we compare, we work out the share of the other ranked items it matches or beats, then average those shares. It only counts the specs listed on the Ladder page.

Source: This site - how we count (our own rule)

starting friction

How much a pad resists when the mouse starts to move from a stop. Dynamic friction is the resistance once it is already moving.

Source: boardzy - 2024 Mousepad Tier List (21:28) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

stock feet

The feet (skates) a mouse comes with from the factory. People replace them when they wear out or to change how the mouse glides.

Source: Wasabi - Beginners Guide to aftermarket skates (0:51) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

stopping power

How easily a mouse pad lets you stop the mouse exactly where you want. Control pads have more of it.

Source: DiamondLobby Reviews - The BEST mouse pad for EVERY GAMER (1:07) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); ARTISAN - NINJA FX ZERO product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

symmetrical shape

The left and right sides of the mouse match, so the shape suits either hand. Side buttons can still be on one side only.

Source: PC Gamer - Best gaming mouse 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); RTINGS review - Logitech G PRO X SUPERLIGHT 2 (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
T5 words: tactile switch, tenkeyless (TKL), tier list, tray mount, tri-mode

tactile switch

A key switch with a small bump you can feel when you press it.

Source: PC Gamer - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

tenkeyless (TKL)

A keyboard without the number pad on the right.

Source: PC Gamer - Best mechanical keyboards 2026 (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

tier list

A YouTube video that sorts products into ranked groups (tiers), such as boardzy's lists. On this site, 'tier' only means a reviewer's own tier list; our own ranking uses ladder groups instead. boardzy says he recommends anything in A tier or above. In boardzy's lists the top group is his "based" tier (the auto-captions often spell it "base tier"). He says A tier and based tier are "more or less the same" and calls both "the top quality options"; the based tier holds his personal favourites, such as ARTISAN pads. His skate list uses the same scale: he says one skate "could be a tier or base" and that "base tiered is based for a reason".

Source: boardzy - 2025 Mouse Skate Tier List (24:19) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); boardzy - 2024 Mousepad Tier List (52:21) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); boardzy - 2024 Mousepad Tier List (52:52) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); boardzy - 2025 Mouse Skate Tier List (20:15) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); boardzy - 2025 Mouse Skate Tier List (20:40) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

tray mount

A common, traditional way of fixing a keyboard's insides to its case. It is the usual alternative to a gasket mount.

Reviewer view: RTINGS describes tray-mounted boards as stiffer than gasket-mounted ones. Opinion: RTINGS

Source: RTINGS review - Ducky OK-M (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); RTINGS review - MonsGeek FUN60 Ultra (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: RTINGS review - Ducky OK-M (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); RTINGS review - MonsGeek FUN60 Ultra (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

tri-mode

A keyboard that connects three ways: a cable, a 2.4 GHz wireless receiver, or Bluetooth.

Source: RTINGS review - MonsGeek FUN60 Ultra (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
U2 words: UHMWPE, USB pass-through

UHMWPE

Another plastic used for mouse feet, instead of PTFE.

Reviewer view: In RTINGS' test of one mouse (the X3 SUPERSTRIKE), its UHMWPE feet glided less smoothly than good PTFE feet. That is one test, not a rule. Opinion: RTINGS

Source: RTINGS review - Logitech G PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
Source for the reviewer view: RTINGS review - Logitech G PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

USB pass-through

A USB port on a pad or keyboard that you can plug another device into. Razer says the Firefly V2 Pro's port can hold your mouse's wireless receiver.

Source: Razer - Firefly V2 Pro product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
W1 word: wired and wireless

wired and wireless

Wired: it plugs into your PC with a cable. Wireless: it connects through a small USB receiver or Bluetooth.

Source: RTINGS review - Corsair KATAR PRO XT (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy); RTINGS review - Logitech MX Master 4 (free sections) (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)
X1 word: XSOFT, SOFT, MID

XSOFT, SOFT, MID

The three base types (firmness levels) ARTISAN sells for the NINJA FX ZERO pad. ARTISAN's own table: XSOFT is very soft, SOFT is moderately soft and MID is firm. MID pads are 3 mm thick; the others are 4 mm.

Source: ARTISAN - NINJA FX ZERO product page (checked 2026-11-18, raw copy)

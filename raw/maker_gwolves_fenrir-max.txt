WHAT IS AN SPDT SWITCH
SPDT = Single Pole Double Throw

SPDT is a faster switch design: the switch signal fires as soon as the spring leaf leaves the contact — you don’t wait for a conventional switch to finish a longer press/travel path. On mouse main buttons, that means an earlier, more direct click trigger — about ~30ms faster than ordinary switches — helping cut click latency.

Triggers on breakaway

When the spring leaf leaves the contact, the switch signal fires immediately — without waiting for a traditional switch’s fuller press/return travel — for a more direct response.

~30ms faster than ordinary switches

Compared with ordinary switch structures, SPDT has a shorter trigger path. Click signals can arrive about ~30ms earlier, helping reduce click latency and speed up rapid-click feedback.

Built for competitive clicking

Product description

The Everglide SU75 Pro HE is a 75% mechanical keyboard designed for gamers who need fast, precise input and customization. It features magnetic Hall Effect switches, which use magnets to detect key presses, reducing wear and increasing reliability. This keyboard suits users who want low latency and advanced gaming functions, with a focus on responsiveness and sound control. It is barebones, requiring you to add your own switches and keycaps.

Key Features
Magnetic Hall Effect switches offer adjustable actuation and durability, improving accuracy and lifespan compared to traditional mechanical switches.
Ultra-low input latency with an 8000Hz polling rate and 0.1ms response time supports fast, precise keystrokes for competitive gaming.
Advanced gaming functions like Rapid Trigger, SOCD cleaning, Dynamic Keystrokes, Multi-Press, and Toggle Key enhance in-game control and key behavior customization.
Hot-swappable magnetic switch sockets allow easy switch replacement without soldering, enabling quick customization or maintenance.
Web-based configuration software requires no installation and lets users remap keys and adjust settings conveniently.
Build Quality and Typing Experience
The CNC-machined aluminum case provides a solid, durable frame with a premium feel and stable typing platform.
Gasket-mounted design, which places a soft layer between the plate and case, reduces typing noise and vibration for a quieter, more comfortable experience.
Multiple layers of sound-dampening materials further improve acoustics, resulting in a deeper, less harsh typing sound.
Dual-zone RGB lighting offers customizable illumination to suit different environments or preferences.
Trade-offs
The keyboard is sold barebones, so you must supply your own switches and keycaps, which may increase setup time and cost.
Only wired connectivity is available, limiting use to desktop or stationary setups.
The 75% layout is compact but may lack dedicated function keys or a numpad, which could be less ideal for users needing extensive key options.
Use Cases
Well suited for competitive gamers who prioritize low latency, precise input, and customizable key behavior.
Good for enthusiasts who want to build a tailored keyboard with Hall Effect switches and value sound control.
Less ideal for users seeking a plug-and-play keyboard or wireless options.
May not fit users who require a full-size layout or extensive dedicated keys for productivity tasks.

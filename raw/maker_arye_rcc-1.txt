The ARYE RCC-1 is a premium lightweight gaming mouse, weighing 46g, crafted from a carbon composite blend of carbon fiber and nylon—twice as strong as standard ABS while remaining lightweight. It features a unique, narrow front-end design, ideal for claw and fingertip grips. Equipped with the most high-end components, offering a DPI up to 32,000 and 8K Hz refresh rate out of the box for an exceptional experience.
Designed by hardcore gamers, the RCC-1 introduces an innovative weight-reduction design, removing material from non-contact areas to reduce weight without sacrificing comfort. It includes integrated side buttons, easily accessible without altering your grip, ensuring a solid grip at all times. The center of mass aligns with the sensor location, helping users intuitively sense the sensor’s position just by feel. A true enthusiast product starts here.
*Each colourway will only be produced once. 2000 units in total.

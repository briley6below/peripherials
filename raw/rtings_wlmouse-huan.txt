Comments

The WLmouse HUAN is a lightweight, symmetrical wireless gaming mouse with a magnesium alloy body. Its body is relatively small with a shape that's nearly identical to the original Razer Viper Mini. It connects wirelessly via its included 8000Hz dongle that's shaped like a cat's face and has a configurable screen that accepts voice commands to change basic settings. It uses the PixArt PAW 3950HS sensor and the Nordic nRF54H20 SoC. You can configure CPI, custom profiles, and other sensor settings through WLmouse's web-based or downloadable configuration software.

Our Verdict
N/AVideo Games (FPS) 

The WLmouse HUAN is an outstanding FPS gaming mouse. It's extremely lightweight and sturdy, with a magnesium alloy chassis and exceptional overall build quality. It features a comfortable, symmetrical shape that's on the smaller side, making it best suited for claw and fingertip grips for most hand sizes. Performance-wise, it delivers exceptionally low click latency and impressive sensor response, with support for polling rates up to 8000Hz.

The WLmouse HUAN is available in Black, White, Silver, Purple, and Pink solid colorways, as well as NEKKO, Cela-don, MA-GIC, PINKKO, and SAKU patterned colorways. We bought and tested the NEKKO colorway. You can see our unit's label here.

Popular Mouse Comparisons

The WLmouse HUAN is a high-end wireless gaming mouse with a magnesium alloy shell. It has a small, symmetrical shape that's nearly identical to the Razer Viper Mini. Its 8k wireless receiver is shaped like a cat and has a screen that can display custom images or accept voice commands to change basic settings. This mouse notably has a solid magnesium alloy upper shell without any weight-saving cutouts, which is particularly uncommon. As a result, it's slightly heavier than mice like the WLmouse Beast X Max, the Finalmouse Starlight-12 Poseidon Small, or even the Razer Viper V4 Pro. That said, it has an exceptional level of build quality that's a cut above any of those options, feeling extremely premium.

Its flagship price point puts it in direct competition with the Viper V4 Pro and the Logitech G PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE. It delivers the same flagship-tier performance as those options, but has a decidedly more premium feel thanks to its build materials. It's worth noting that you can achieve the same level of raw performance with mid-range and budget options like the Scyrox V6 or the MCHOSE L7 Pro, but if you're in the market for a luxuriously well-built mouse that uses exotic materials, the WLmouse is an excellent option.

For more recommendations, see our picks for the best gaming mice, the best lightweight mice, and the best wireless gaming mice.

WLmouse Beast X
SEARCHAmazon

The WLmouse HUAN and the WLmouse Beast X are wireless gaming mice from the same manufacturer. The HUAN has a new sensor and supports a higher maximum polling rate of 8000Hz compared to the Beast X's 4000Hz, but this distinction doesn't materialize into performance gains. Both mice feature magnesium upper shells, but the Beast X has large cutouts throughout the body, while the HUAN's shell is completely solid. As a result, the HUAN has a much more solid and premium-feeling build, but the Beast X is about 10 grams lighter.

Razer Viper V4 Pro

The Razer Viper V4 Pro and the WLmouse HUAN are both outstanding lightweight wireless gaming mice. Both deliver exceptional wireless performance that's suitable for gaming at any competitive level. While the two have similar symmetrical shapes, the WLmouse is smaller, making it better suited to smaller hand sizes. The WLmouse is also made of a magnesium alloy, giving it an exceptionally high-quality, solid feel compared to the Razer's plastic shell.

Logitech G PRO X SUPERLIGHT 2

The WLmouse HUAN and the Logitech G PRO X SUPERLIGHT 2 are exceptionally good wireless gaming mice. Both deliver superb raw performance that's suitable for gaming at any competitive level. The WLmouse is lighter and has better build quality as its upper shell is made of a magnesium alloy compared to the Logitech's plastic construction. That said, the Logitech's medium size is likely better suited for a variety of hand sizes since the WLmouse is on the smaller side.

Hitscan Hyperlight
SEE PRICEHitscan

The Hitscan Hyperlight and the WLmouse HUAN are lightweight wireless gaming mice. Both mice have fairly similar symmetrical shapes that are on the smaller side, but the Hitscan is slightly longer, making it more accommodating for larger hands. The WLmouse has better build quality as it's made of a magnesium alloy, but the Hitscan's plastic shell weighs less, making it easier to aim and flick. Both mice have superb raw performance.

Would be nice to see click latency at the various polling rates (ie 1000/2000/4000/8000). Also battery testing; maybe some sort of test where the mouse is mounted to a 3 axis machine and moved around constantly at random directions and speeds to keep manufacturers honest.

Hi, thanks for your comment!

We mostly test click latency at the maximum wired and wireless polling rates as we want to identify the lowest possible latency of the mouse where it matters most which is usually in a gaming scenario. We sometimes do test the click latency at other polling rates for some mice and leave a note in the text of the box when we make some interesting findings such as a marginal difference between 4000 Hz and 8000 Hz. Otherwise, the indicated latency is the lowest measured and we consider that it is what’s most important for someone who needs to use the mouse in a gaming context.

Regarding the development of a test to evaluate how long a battery lasts, we will keep this in mind as it would effectively be interesting to determine if the specs given by the manufacturer are close to the reality.

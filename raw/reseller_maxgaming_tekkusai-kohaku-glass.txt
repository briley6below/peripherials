PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

Mousepad
 from 
Tekkusai

Kohaku Glass Mouse Pad is a designed mouse pad that offers a premium experience with its hardened glass. It has a size of 500 x 450 mm and a thickness of 2 mm, which contributes to stability and durability. With a nano-coating that reduces friction and stickiness during use, the product is aimed at gamers and professional users who require precision and quick response.

The mouse pad is equipped with a silicone base that extends all the way to the edges, ensuring it stays firmly in place on the surface. The rounded design of the corners and edges prevents the mat from being damaged during use. The surface of the mat allows for smooth and quick mouse movements, which is crucial for gaming and design work. The special coating helps to keep hands in place and improves comfort during longer usage sessions.

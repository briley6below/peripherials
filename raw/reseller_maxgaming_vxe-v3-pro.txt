PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

Wireless
 from 
VXE

V3 Pro is a lightweight gaming mouse from VXE that offers high precision and responsiveness. It is designed for gamers who prioritize performance and comfort in an ergonomic form factor. Equipped with the PAW 3395SE sensor, it provides an optimal gaming experience with accurate detection of movements. Weighing only 49±3g and sized to fit small to medium hands, it is easy to handle during long gaming sessions.

This gaming mouse is equipped with a powerful MCU, BS20, that delivers a polling rate of 1K in both wired and wireless modes. The refined matte surface and new nano-coating contribute to a superior feel and durability, making the mouse withstand stresses from intense gaming battles. Its dimensions, 116.6 × 62.15 × 38.2 mm, are carefully designed to provide a good grip while maintaining a low weight for smooth maneuverability. With these specifications, the V3 Pro is a wise choice for the serious gamer looking for a reliable mouse.

BRAND

VXE Studio is a gaming accessories brand focused on high-performance hardware for competitive players and enthusiasts. The company was founded by gamers with the ambition of creating equipment they themselves would want to use, combining advanced technology with modern industrial design to deliver precision, speed, and comfort.

With the philosophy “Extreme Evolution,” VXE Studio continuously works to develop and refine its technology to meet the demands of today’s players. The brand first gained attention with the VXE Dragonfly R1 gaming mouse, known for its combination of design, performance, and value. Through collaborations with partners such as VGNLab, the company has also developed products like the VXE75 mechanical aluminum keyboard, which combines a premium feel, aesthetics, and high functionality.

THE WINNING CLICK
PRO X3 SUPERSTRIKE is an evolution of your favorite weapon. Built on the innovative Haptic Inductive Trigger System (HITS), it’s the first PRO Series mouse to feature the new flagship NxLOGI chipset. X3 is lighter & more balanced, has longer battery life, and improved wireless performance.

A revolution in record-breaking click speed. SUPERSTRIKE mice replace traditional microswitches with HITS Technology, reducing click latency by up to 30 ms via custom actuation & reset points.

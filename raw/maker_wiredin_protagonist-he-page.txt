WHAT'S YOUR STORY?

INSPIRED BY GROWTH.

Built around the original Protagonist's signature "Keyframe" system, the PHE is designed to grow with you as we expand the ecosystem. The design has been refined — edges sharpened, lines tightened — as the culmination of Wired In's neo-industrial brutalist aesthetic.

FILE 02 / 08

NEVER SETTLE

A TRADITIONAL THREE-PIECE DESIGN

Despite a more accessible price point, the PHE doesn't pull any punches. Full Keyframe top case, bottom case, and weight — paired with two mounting solutions out of the box.

FILE 03 / 08

MOUNTING SOLUTIONS

TWO MOUNTS · ONE BOARD

Two distinct mounting solutions ship in every PHE box. Firm isolated top mount, or the softer gasket mount shown here. Pick the feel — no additional purchase, no compromise.

TOP DOWN · GASKET FLEX · 3.0S LOOP
PHE.001 · GASKET MOUNT · WIRED IN //

FILE 04 / 08

ENGRAVING

ENGRAVED · NOT INLAID.

The Wired In mark on the bottom case isn't a separate piece pressed in. It's high-gloss engraving cut directly into anodized aluminum — same technique applied matte on the rear of the weight.

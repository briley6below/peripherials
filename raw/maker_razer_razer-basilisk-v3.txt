FAQS
What is the form factor of the Razer Basilisk V3?
The Razer Basilisk V3 features a unique right-handed ergonomic design with a signature thumb rest for less finger drag for smoother flicks when gaming.
How do I remap buttons and customize settings for the Razer Basilisk V3?
In order to configure button mappings, macros, lighting effects and more, you’ll have to first download and install Razer Synapse 3.
What are macros and how do I assign one to a Razer mouse button?
A macro is a recording that contains a series of inputs/commands. In a situation where you would normally have to press multiple buttons to execute different actions, a macro allows you to execute all these actions in sequence with just one button. The ability to record and assign a macro to any programmable button is one of the best features of Razer gaming mice.
See How can I assign macros to my Razer mouse buttons for more details.
Is the multi-function trigger button on the left-side of the Razer Basilisk V3 removeable?
The multi-function trigger of the Razer Basilisk V3 is not removeable and is optimally positioned for easy access without accidental misclicks when gaming.
What does the button at the bottom of the Razer Basilisk V3 do?
By default, this button allows you to toggle across different onboard profiles saved onto your mouse. With Razer Synapse 3, you can reconfigure this like any other button on the mouse.
How can I take full advantage of the 10 programmable buttons on the Razer Basilisk V3?
We highly recommend downloading Razer Synapse and enabling Razer HyperShift—an advanced feature that allows you to activate a secondary button profile on the mouse at the press of a key. This means you can practically double the amount of inputs on the Razer Basilisk V3 as if the mouse had 20 buttons.
How do I optimize my mouse for the best accuracy in gaming?
To improve accuracy, consider disabling the "Enhance pointer precision" option in your Windows mouse settings, as this feature can affect mouse sensitivity. Additionally, you can further optimize performance using the Razer Synapse app. Learn more about our enhanced DPI features.

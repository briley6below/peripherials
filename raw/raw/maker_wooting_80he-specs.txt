Sound & Feel

To refine the sound and feel, we've built the Wooting 80HE with a silicone gasket mount, a PC switch plate and screw-in stabilizers.

Silicone Gasket Mount

The PCBA and switch plate are held together by a silicone gasket. This absorbs impact, resulting in less noise and a softer feel when you type.

PC Switch Plate

The Polycarbonate (PC) switch plate softens the overall typing experience by muting more of the higher frequencies and placing an emphasis on "thock".

Screw-in Stabilizers

The Wooting 80HE is equipped with screw-in stabilizers to eliminate rattle. They're also replaceable with most 3rd party screw-in and clip-in stabilizers.

Ergonomics

We design our keyboards to have the lowest possible front height with the lowest possible angle to help reduce strain and slow down muscle fatigue. Depending on your hand size, you can change the angle to your preference using the interchangeable silicone feet.

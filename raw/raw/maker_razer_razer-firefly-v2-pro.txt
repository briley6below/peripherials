FAQS
Is the Razer Firefly V2 Pro a hard or soft mat?

The Razer Firefly V2 Pro is a hard mat.

Will the mat’s RGB backlit surface interfere with a mouse’s optical sensor?

No. The Razer Firefly V2 Pro comes equipped with a micro-textured surface that is optimized for optical sensors, allowing for quick swipes and precise tracking at all times.

How do I customize the lighting zones on the Razer Firefly V2 Pro?

The Razer Firefly V2 Pro’s lighting zones can be customized via Chroma Studio in Razer Synapse. The possibilities are endless—choose from a selection of preset effects or customize individual RGB zones with numerous profiles and settings to your liking.

How does the Razer Firefly V2 Pro differ from the Razer Firefly V2?

The Razer Firefly V2 Pro has an edge-to-edge backlit design that illuminates the whole mat, whereas the Razer Firefly V2 features all-round edge lighting.

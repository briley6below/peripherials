KATAR PRO XT

LIGHTWEIGHT DESIGN
HEAVY-WEIGHT PERFORMANCE

ULTRA-LIGHT &
AMAZINGLY AGILE

Weighing just 73g, the KATAR PRO XT is light and agile for hours of fast-paced FPS or MOBA gameplay. The compact, symmetric shape makes it great for claw and fingertip grip styles. The drag-reducing paracord cable enables faster, tighter, and more precise mouse movements.

GAME-WINNING PRECISION

CORSAIR QUICKSTRIKE buttons use a spring-loaded design, delivering zero gap between the left and right click buttons and their switches, so your clicks, shots, and spells are faster than ever.

18K DPI OPTICAL SENSOR

The precision and high-accuracy tracking you need for victory, customizable in 1 DPI steps with 3 DPI presets.

ULTRA-LIGHT &
AMAZINGLY AGILE

Weighing just 73g, the KATAR PRO XT is light and agile for hours of fast-paced FPS or MOBA gameplay. The compact, symmetric shape makes it great for claw and fingertip grip styles. The drag-reducing paracord cable enables faster, tighter, and more precise mouse movements.

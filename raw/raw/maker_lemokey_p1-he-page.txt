LEMOKEY P1 HE

Introducing the P1 HE, a revolutionary tool designed for gamers who demand precision, speed, and customization. This keyboard is equipped with advanced features, including Gateron double-rail magnetic switches, a knob design, a gasket structure, advanced acoustic foams, and double-shot PBT keycaps, all of which enhance your gaming experience and elevate your performance.

Lemokey P1 HE, a brand of Keychron, introduces a 2.4G connection mode with a superfast 1000Hz polling rate, ensuring a lightning-fast gaming experience. Whether you’re a casual gamer or a competitive player, the P1 HE is designed to meet your needs and provide the ultimate comfort and control.

EXTREME STABILITY AND ULTRA-RESPONSIVE TRIGGER
Gateron Double-Rail Magnetic Switches

Experience the cutting-edge pre-lubed Gateron double-rail magnetic switches that deliver an ultra-smooth, ultra-stable, and responsive typing experience, ensuring that every keystroke is registered with precision. With industry-leading lightning-fast response times, you gain precise control over the keyboard's feel and performance.

As opposed to traditional mechanical switches, magnetic switches use pressure-sensitive hall effect sensors to detect keypresses—which means you get to set the depth at which you want each keypress to engage, with 0.1 millimeter precision.

Adjustable Actuation Point

Tailor each key to your liking by fine-tuning actuation points that range from 0.2 to 3.8 mm, with a sensitivity of 0.1 mm. Configure shallow key presses for quick actions and deeper presses for intentional engagement, reducing the likelihood of accidental touches. This feature enables gamers to optimize their response times, making it easier to execute rapid actions in fast-paced games.

Dynamic Rapid Trigger

Keys can be activated or deactivated based on their travel distance rather than fixed points. They engage immediately upon pressing and disengage as soon as you release them, enabling instant repetition of keystrokes in a fluid motion. This feature allows you to execute a sequence of actions quickly and efficiently during gameplay, making it especially beneficial in gaming scenarios where rapid reflexes are essential.

Last Keystroke Prioritization (LKP) & Snap Click

LKP enables seamless directional changes by prioritizing the last key pressed, ensuring smoother gameplay in fast-paced titles like Valorant. Snap Click intelligently manages simultaneous key presses by favoring deeper keypresses, automatically resetting shallower ones.

Together, LKP and Snap Click create a powerful combination that enhances the P1 HE keyboard’s performance, making it an essential tool for gamers who seek precision and responsiveness.

*They are disabled by default, but you can easily enable them in our Launcher web app when needed.

Multiple Actions, One Keystroke

Simplify your gameplay or workflow by assigning four actions to a single key based on how deeply you press it. For instance, a light press can make your character walk forward in a game, while a deeper press will make them run. Additionally, releasing the key supports two-stage settings. This feature allows you to execute complex commands effortlessly, streamlining your gaming strategy.

Analog Mode

Switch to analog mode for a more immersive experience, allowing for nuanced control in games that support it. This mode provides variable inputs instead of the usual on/off signals, enabling more fluid and dynamic control while enhancing your ability to perform actions with precision.

With each keypress, the P1 HE detects how far you press, translating that into varying speeds and intensities—perfect for games and sports simulations that require precise movements and fine-tuning.

ULTRA-LOW LATENCY CONNECTION
2.4 GHz Wireless With 1000 Hz Polling Rate

Designed with pro-grade 2.4 GHz wireless connectivity and a 1000 Hz polling rate, the P1 HE offers ultra-low latency performance and fast response, crucial for gaming and real-time applications. Plus, it can support up to 3 device wireless connections with on-the-fly switching through stable Bluetooth 5.2 mode for a seamless gaming experience that will keep you ahead of the competition.

FULL METAL BODY
Robust And Refine Art Piece

Crafted for durability and aesthetics, the full metal body enhances the keyboard's robustness and imparts a sleek, premium appearance. This high-quality material ensures structural integrity and stability, even during intense gaming sessions, providing a solid and reliable base for every keystroke.

MIRROR POLISH FINISH

The back of the keyboard features a mirror-polished strip that boasts a highly reflective, smooth finish and a scratch-free appearance.

ENHANCED ACOUSTIC FOAMS
Satisfying Sound And Feel

By combining 3 mm thick acoustic foam, 2.5 mm thick EPDM foam, and PET film, the P1 HE effectively reduces the vibrations caused by keystrokes, resulting in a more satisfying sound and feel.

GASKET MOUNT DESIGN
Comfortable Typing Experience

Gasket silicone beans are installed on the keyboard PCB to reduce acoustic resonance. This mounting method enhances the keyboard's flexibility and provides a cushioned feel, making the P1 HE more comfortable and consistent during extended typing and gaming sessions.

EPIC CORE
Powerful Chip (MCU) With 256K Flash

Engineered with a powerful 256K flash chip (MCU) and QMK open-source firmware, this keyboard offers enhanced flexibility for developers. The polling rate is set at 1000 Hz out of the box in both Type-C wired mode and 2.4 GHz wireless mode, significantly improving performance during competitive gaming.

HOT-SWAPPABLE
Customize Your Typing Experience

Unlike mechanical switches that rely on physical contact, Hall Effect magnetic switches use magnets to detect key presses. You can easily customize your keyboard to suit your preferences or replace worn-out switches with compatible Hall Effect magnetic switches.

*The P1 HE is compatible with the magnetic switches sold on the Keychron and Lemokey website only.

DOUBLE-SHOT PBT KEYCAPS
Greater Durability

Durable keycaps are essential for gaming keyboards, significantly affecting your keystroke experience. The P1 HE provides two keycap options to cater to different preferences and needs. Both options feature premium double-shot Cherry profile PBT keycaps that are durable, oil-resistant, and wear-resistant, ensuring optimal gaming performance throughout the keyboard's lifespan.

Cherry Profile Shine-Through Double-Shot PBT Keycaps

Cherry Profile Non-Shine-Through Double-Shot PBT Keycaps

NORTH-FACING RGB
Unlock Dynamic Backlit Control

It comes with 22 preset RGB backlight settings. You can further fine-tune the RGB hue, saturation, brightness, effect speed, and different modes and create more of your own unique style by remapping these keys to unlock vivid dynamic RGB lighting control.

Cherry Profile Shine-Through Double-Shot PBT Keycap Version

NEWLY ENHANCED STABILIZERS
Ultimate Precision And Stability

Our upgraded stabilizers now feature reinforced sidewalls around the stem, providing enhanced protection and significantly improving stability. These "walls" create a stronger connection between the switches and keycaps, ensuring smoother and more reliable typing on larger keys such as the spacebar, shift, enter, and backspace.

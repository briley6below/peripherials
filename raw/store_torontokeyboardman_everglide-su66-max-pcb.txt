Description
EVERGLIDE · SU66 MAX
A 600 MHz magnetic keyboard PCB for the GH60 platform.

Everglide's flagship magnetic keyboard PCB module for the GH60 form factor — Hall Effect (magnetic) switch support, 600 MHz high-frequency MCU, 32,000 Hz scan rate, 8000 Hz polling, and 0.001 mm Rapid Trigger precision. Split spacebar layout, surround underglow lighting, and a complete tray-mount stack-up included in the box.

600 MHZ MCU
High-frequency chip. Sub-millisecond response.

The SU66 MAX runs on a 600 MHz high-frequency MCU paired with a high-speed scan matrix — driving a 0.06 ms input latency, 32 kHz scan rate, and 8 kHz polling end-to-end. That's the headroom magnetic switches need for Rapid Trigger to actually behave like Rapid Trigger.

SURROUND UNDERGLOW
Full RGB top, ring of light below.

136 per-key RGB LEDs on top, 38 underglow LEDs wrapping the rear of the PCB, plus 10 dedicated LEDs behind the spacebar. Bright, consistent, fully addressable through the driver.

SPLIT SPACEBAR
Split-space layout, GH60-compatible.

Split spacebar for the player who wants a dedicated layer trigger under the right thumb. Drops directly into any GH60-compatible case — no custom plate work, no daughterboard relocation, no rewiring. Build the magnetic keyboard you actually want.

WEB DRIVER
Browser-based. No installer.

Configure Rapid Trigger, per-key actuation point, magnetic-switch calibration, lighting, and macros through the web driver at xsyd.top — open the page, plug in, configure. No installer, no system service.

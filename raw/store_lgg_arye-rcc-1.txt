Product Description:
The ARYE RCC-1 is a premium lightweight gaming mouse, weighing 46g, crafted froma carbon composite blend of carbon fiber and nylon—twice as strong as standardABS while remaining lightweight. It features a unique, narrow front-end design, idealfor claw and fingertip grips. Equipped with the most high-end components, offeringa DPI up to 32,000 and 8K Hz refresh rate out of the box for an exceptionalexperience.Designed by hardcore gamers, the RCC-1 introduces an innovative weight-reductiondesign, removing material from non-contact areas to reduce weight withoutsacrificing comfort. It includes integrated side buttons, easily accessible withoutaltering your grip, ensuring a solid grip at all times. The center of mass aligns withthe sensor location, helping users intuitively sense the sensor’s position just by feel.A true enthusiast product starts here.

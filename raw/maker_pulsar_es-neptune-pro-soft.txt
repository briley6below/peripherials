Combined with the SlimFlex base, the Neptune Pro ensures a stable yet fluid motion, delivering the precise responsiveness required for high-intensity competitive gaming.

XSOFT

BRAKING POWER : MOST

GLIDE SPEED : SLOWEST

Compresses easily even under light pressure, creating a slight “cup” around the skates. Higher breakaway force (slower to get moving) and Higher stopping power (easier to stop precisely). Extra soft has the most stopping power for flick heavy games and the most comfort due to the almost memory-foam-like feel.

SOFT

BRAKING POWER : BALANCED
GLIDE SPEED : BALANCED

Moderate compression with a well-rounded feel. Balanced launch, glide, and braking. Popular and approachable for most players and playstyles. Soft has a good balance between comfort, stopping power when you need it, and tracking as well.

FIRM

BRAKING POWER : LEAST
GLIDE SPEED : FASTEST


Minimal compression; skates don’t “dig in” to the surface. Lower breakaway force (quicker start) and higher glide speed. Favors long tracking/continuous motion (tracking-heavy games). Firm has little to no give to it for the most consistent performance in tracking heavy games.

Adjustable Actuation Point

Adjust actuation point on the fly. Go shallow for lightning-fast taps in FPS games, or deeper for deliberate control in MOBAs and RPGs. Unleash your speed exactly when you need it.

Dynamic Rapid Trigger

Cancel keystrokes instantly. The faster you release, the sooner you can fire again.
No more waiting for full key reset—our dynamic rapid trigger detects downward and upward movement in real-time, slashing the delay between actions. Perfect for rapid-fire shots, jitter-building, and any scenario where milliseconds separate victory from defeat.

Last Keystroke Prioritization (LKP) & Snap Click

LKP ensures your latest directional input always takes precedence, enabling instant, seamless movement changes─crucial for outmaneuvering opponents in games like Valorant.

Snap Click intelligently handles simultaneous key presses by prioritizing deeper actuations and automatically resetting shallower ones, eliminating input conflicts and unintended commands.

Full Body CNC Aluminum

220-Mesh Anodizing Aluminum Case

The full-body CNC aluminum case delivers exceptional rigidity and a premium feel. The 220‑mesh hollow design creates a distinctive open aesthetic. Each case is precision‑machined and anodized, housing LEDs that shine through the mesh for a striking gaming atmosphere.

174 LEDs, North and South RGB

LED Lighting System

The PCB features 174 LEDs in total. Each switch is illuminated by dual LEDs (top and bottom positions) for even, vibrant per-key lighting. The spacebar stands out with 10 dedicated LEDs for a bold underglow effect. Additionally, the bottom of the case houses 38 LEDs, creating a stunning ambient glow that enhances your gaming setup.

Magnetic Quick-Release | Gasket Mount

Snap on. Pull off. Instantly.

A magnetic quick-release aluminum shell – no screws, just a gentle pull to open. Cleaning, modding, or swapping switches feels like handling a finely crafted instrument. Inside, the gasket mount suspends the plate on elastic pads, giving every keystroke a soft, cushioned landing and a clean, focused sound. No harsh bottom-outs – just that satisfying, bouncy feel that makes you want to type a little longer. Your fingers will fall in love first.

3 Keys Split Spacebar

One spacebar. Triple the control.

The 3-key split spacebar (2.25u + 1.75u + 2.25u) breaks a single long bar into three independent keys right under your thumbs. Keep the middle as space, then assign the sides to Backspace, Shift, layer toggle, or in-game macros like jump-crouch. It’s a productivity and gaming hack built directly into your layout. Once your thumbs taste this kind of freedom, a regular spacebar will just feel like wasted potential.

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

Wireless
 from 
ARYE

RCC-1 is a wireless gaming mouse from ARYE with a weight of only 46 grams. The mouse is made of a carbon fiber and nylon composite, making it twice as strong as standard ABS and very lightweight. It features a unique, narrow front design that is optimal for claw and fingertip grip. RCC-1 offers a DPI of up to 32,000 and a polling rate of 8K Hz, providing high precision and responsiveness during gameplay.

ARYE RCC-1 has an innovative weight-reducing design that removes material from contact-free areas, increasing comfort without compromising on lightness. The mouse has integrated side buttons that are easily accessible without changing grip, ensuring that gamers always maintain a firm hold. The center of mass is adjusted to the sensor position, making it easier for users to intuitively feel the sensor location.

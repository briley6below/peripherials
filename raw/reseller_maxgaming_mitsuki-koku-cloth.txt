PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

Mousepad
 from 
MITSUKI

A high-quality mouse pad designed for gaming and professional use. It is made of polyester woven fabric and has a surface that provides good control and precision. It is aimed at gamers and computer users looking for a reliable and functional solution for their gaming and workstations. Only 1,000 units will be produced. This product will not return to stock in the future.

This mouse pad is equipped with a SlimFlex Poron-based design, providing a soft feel and flexibility during use. The material is intended to provide a long-lasting and smooth surface for mouse movements while minimizing friction. The dimensions of the mouse pad are 490 x 420 x 4 mm, providing an optimal size for both gaming and work. It also features low-profile stitched edges for increased durability. The mouse pad can withstand intense use without losing its qualities.

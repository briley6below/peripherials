Who we listen to

Reviewers

The 8 review outlets behind every opinion on this site, and who they are.

The short answer

  • Every opinion on this site comes from one of these 8 outlets: RTINGS, boardzy, Tom's Hardware, PC Gamer, DiamondLobby, Wasabi, PCGamesN, Tom's Guide.
  • Lab reviews publish test results; best-of lists rank products; video reviewers give hands-on views. Stores and maker pages are used only for specs and prices.
  • RTINGS is cited most, in 27 items.
  • 13 more reviewers are on our reading list but not used yet, so they do not affect anything here.

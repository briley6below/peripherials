What the labels and badges mean

Labels on sentences

Agreed
Two or more different outlets say it.
Opinion: NAME
Only one outlet says it.
Fact
A spec, price or test result from a maker page or a lab test (RTINGS). No opinion in it.
Reported: NAME
A plain fact, not an opinion, but only a review site or a store states it. No maker page or lab test confirms it yet.
Unverified
We could not confirm it. It is not used for ranking, filters or "cheapest".

Badges on cards

80+60+40+<40
Mice and keyboards only: our spec score out of 100. It counts only 2 or 3 gaming specs, not comfort, shape or features. It is not an overall grade.
21
Mousepads and skates only: how many outlets recommend it. A number, not a grade.
✕
A reviewer says to avoid it.
?
Not ranked: a spec we compare is missing or not comparable.
W
Built for work, so not ranked on gaming specs.

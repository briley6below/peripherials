What the labels and badges mean

Labels on sentences

Agreed
Two or more different outlets say it.
Opinion: NAME
Only one outlet says it.
Fact
A spec, price or test result from a maker page or a lab test (RTINGS). No opinion in it.
Reported: NAME
A plain fact, not an opinion, but only a review site or a store states it. No maker page or lab test confirms it yet.
Unverified
We could not confirm it. It is not used for ranking, filters or "cheapest".

4 quick questions

Four questions to narrow things down. Everything below comes from the item data and its sources.

1. What do you mostly do?

  • Fast shooters (FPS games): lighter mice and higher polling rates score higher on our spec ladder; on keyboards, magnetic switches with Rapid Trigger count as a plus.
  • Work and everyday use: these are built for it: Logitech MX Master 4 and Logitech MX Keys S.
  • Not sure: start with the items most outlets recommend, in the short answer above.
